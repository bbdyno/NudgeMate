NudgeMate does not require an account or sign-in.

Core review flow:
1. Complete onboarding and optionally grant Calendar and Notification permissions.
2. The Today tab shows seeded or user-created preparation items and recurring rhythms.
3. Open Settings using the gear button, then select NudgeMate Pro to review the monthly, yearly, and lifetime purchase options.
4. Widgets can be added from the iOS widget gallery. A Live Activity starts when a preparation item is moved to In Progress and Live Activities are allowed in system settings.

Calendar titles, dates, rhythms, and preparation items are processed on device and are never sent to the advertising SDK. Free users may see one Google Mobile Ads interstitial after completing Daily Recap, limited to once per 24 hours. Consent is managed with Google's User Messaging Platform, and privacy choices can be reopened in Settings where required. Pro users do not initialize or request ads. StoreKit handles all purchases. The app provides Restore Purchases and Manage Subscription controls in the paywall.
