NudgeMate helps you anticipate recurring tasks and prepare for important dates before they become urgent.

It finds recurring candidates in the Apple calendars you choose, then saves only the rhythms you confirm. For important dates, adaptive check-ins respond to Not Ready, In Progress, and Ready so your next action stays clear.

Highlights
• Suggestions for when recurring tasks may be due
• Preparation trackers with a clear next action
• Timely notifications, widgets, and Live Activities
• Daily Recap for a calm end-of-day review
• JSON data export and complete local deletion

Privacy comes first. Calendar and preparation information is processed on your device and is not sent to an external server. NudgeMate uses no advertising, external analytics SDK, or cross-app tracking.

The free version includes unlimited manual rhythms, up to three adaptive rhythms, and one active preparation tracker. NudgeMate Pro removes the adaptive rhythm and preparation limits. Choose a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription, or a lifetime purchase. Subscriptions can be managed or cancelled at any time in your App Store account settings.
